I decided to do this project while reading the book "Large Language Models: The Hard Parts" while being unemployed :D

Before, I have worked as an Instructional Designer for 3+ years. 
I did my job perfectly, I was so commited to what I used to do that in 3 years I have produced over 200 lessons in different disciplines, mainly in Computing Vertical. 
Moreover, the quality of the lessons had been developing with years. 

One day, we used OpenAI platform, and instead of creating a LLM for our students, we just attached many many lessons to the "agent", wrote down the instructions, and connected it to the platform.

Two years later, I decided to create a small LLM for the imaginary company who produce computing courses for K12 students. 
This model is designed for students and aimed to help them to achieve new knowledge heights while being safe from the infromation that any open source LLM model can leak. 

I was given the policy. The steps are:
1. Generate synthetic dataset of policy alighned outputs
2. To apply DPO to finetune the model
3. Evaluate the base model and fine tuned one and measure consistency against the olicy
